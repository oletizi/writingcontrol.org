# The Big Lie and the Rogue Return

The entire program is a giant con. The ships will never reach a destination. It's just a scheme to move people off-world — both for some insane, delusional population engineering, and so the program architects can skim off the top.

But in order to recruit more people, the friends and family left behind need to talk to those who left, so they'll be incentivized to sign up for future launches. Some skeptics will demand mTLS-style encryption and secure identity mechanisms for the conversations. The communication will be live and real… for a while.

Eventually the conversations with the departed get surreptitiously replaced by AI. It isn't clear the departed stay alive. They are definitely replaced by deep fakes.

But: the crew of one ship gets wise to the con and figures out a way to turn the ship around. The program masterminds try to destroy it. The rogue ship manages to get a message back to Earth — "It's a cookbook!" style.
