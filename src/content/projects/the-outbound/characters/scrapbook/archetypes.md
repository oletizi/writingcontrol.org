# Character Archetypes

- **Strivers.** Those on the privileged end of the scale with intense FOMO. Rilly rilly want to get on the spaceship.
- **Billionaires.** Grifters exploiting the Strivers. Two camps. (TBD: which two.)
- **Outsiders.** Don't even think about the spaceship. Barely live in the built world.
- **Mavens / Wizards / Cassandras.** See the machine and its mechanisms for what they are. Powerless (or uninterested) to stop them. Thread through the interstices, surviving via arbitrage. Choose not to be grifters because they don't have giant wounds to bandage with money and adulation.
